## Notes

### BSc Thesis: 
* My thesis is about evaluating the design and performance of underground support in Tunnel 3 of Sigli, Banda Aceh. I classified the rock mass characterization using available datasets, translating them to be used in Finite Element Modelling (RS2). The results of my thesis show the deformation, rock support interaction of the surrounding rock mass to the existence of the tunnel.

### Deep Geology Waste Repository: 
* This document was created as a final assignment for the Environment and Geotechnical Engineering course. The project aimed to assess the behavior of underground excavations for a deep geological repository for nuclear waste. The construction of the repository alters the surrounding stress field, while heat generated by the nuclear waste affects the temperature and thermal stresses within the surrounding rock. Using a finite element model in PLAXIS, I developed a coupled model to analyze the resulting changes in stress and deformation caused by the construction and operation of the deep geological repository.
